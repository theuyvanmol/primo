The Grand Ho Tram & The Bluffs

Resort & golf hospitality

Market the destination and the decision

Travel and golf audiences are choosing an experience as well as a place. The Grand Ho Tram and The Bluffs provide a context where imagery, destination information and the reason to visit all matter. Marketing needs to express that experience while helping the visitor understand what to explore next.

Consider the audience’s market and intent

A local leisure guest and an international golfer may arrive with different questions. Audience planning, language and campaign context should reflect those differences. Clear destination content helps make the marketing useful before the booking stage, while a consistent message keeps the brand experience connected across channels.

Bring creative and paid media into one plan

We approach destination marketing by connecting the creative idea with the intended audience, the landing page and the enquiry journey. This creates a practical basis for deciding which content to develop, where campaigns should lead and which customer actions should inform the next review.

The priorities behind the experience

  • Destination storytelling and hospitality imagery
  • Audience planning for relevant markets
  • Consistent campaign and landing-page journeys
  • Measurement connected with customer actions
