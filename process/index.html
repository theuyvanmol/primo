OUR PROCESS

From insight to impact.

A clear process gives ambitious ideas a practical route into the world. Here is how we connect the brief, the work and the learning that follows.

Discuss your project ↗Explore services
PRIMO / STRATEGY · CREATIVE · TECHNOLOGY

Four stages. One shared direction.

01

Discover

Understand what the business needs before choosing what to make.

We begin with the audience, the offer and the current experience. We look at the existing website, content, campaigns and customer journey to understand what is already working and where the gaps are. The outcome is a shared brief with a clear objective, useful constraints and the questions the project needs to answer.

02

Strategise

Turn the findings into a practical direction.

We define the message, priorities and role of each touchpoint. This might include the site structure, a content direction, a campaign plan or a workflow map. The strategy connects the creative work with the intended customer action. Deliverables, approvals and a review rhythm are agreed so the team understands how the project will move.

03

Create

Make the brand and the journey work together.

Design, content and development take shape around the agreed direction. We review the work as an experience rather than separate outputs: the ad should fit the landing page, the website should support the message and the content should help the audience understand the offer. Feedback is organised around the brief and the decisions that matter.

04

Grow

Use the work, learn from it and improve what comes next.

After launch, the focus moves to observation and refinement. We review agreed measures, collect useful feedback and identify the next priority. A campaign may need new creative, a website may need a clearer page or a workflow may need a better handover. Growth comes from a repeatable cycle of useful action and informed review.

What makes collaboration work

Clear decisions are as important as creative ideas.

We agree who contributes, who approves and which information is needed at each stage. A useful brief, a shared source of feedback and a realistic review rhythm help keep the work moving.

The scope can be focused or ongoing. What matters is that the team understands the next decision, the reason behind it and the responsibilities on both sides. That creates room for a stronger creative result without losing the practical details.
