Start with a useful workflow, then choose the technology.

Find the friction before adding another tool

Automation is most useful when it solves a specific repeated problem. We map how information moves between your team, campaigns and reporting before proposing a new workflow. The first opportunity might be organising enquiries, preparing a reporting draft or keeping a content calendar up to date. Clear inputs and responsibilities matter more than an impressive collection of tools.

Keep people in the decisions that matter

AI can support research, drafts and organisation, but output still needs context and review. We consider where human approval belongs, which information a tool can access and how the team checks the result. A practical workflow makes those boundaries visible. It should be understandable enough to maintain when the person who first set it up is not available.

Explore Orbit as a connected working environment

Orbit is PRIMO’s marketing-platform concept: a place to bring reporting, tasks and team workflows closer together. The current website shows an illustrative interface with sample data. Live channel connections, account access and production automation need separate configuration and testing. We use the concept to discuss which parts of the marketing process would benefit from a more connected view.

What the scope can include

For teams with repeated marketing or reporting tasks that need a clearer process before scaling their use of AI.

  • Workflow and tool review
  • Automation opportunity mapping
  • Human review and approval design
  • Prototype or pilot planning
  • Integration scope and handover guidance

Questions before we begin

Is the Orbit dashboard on this website live?

No. The current Orbit interface is a concept using sample data. It does not connect to your advertising or social accounts.

Can automation replace our marketing team?

The work is designed to support the team. Strategy, approval, client context and creative judgement still need people.

Where should we start?

Choose one recurring task with clear inputs, a useful output and an owner. A small pilot is easier to evaluate and improve than a broad automation promise.
