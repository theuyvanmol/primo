PRIMO / STRATEGY · CREATIVE · TECHNOLOGY

A website should create confidence and help people act.

Design around the visitor’s next question

People arrive with different levels of knowledge. Some are discovering your brand; others already want a menu, a service detail or a way to contact you. We structure pages around those needs, with clear navigation and content that earns attention. The goal is a site that feels considered and makes sense before a visitor has to think about how to use it.

Give the brand room to move

Art direction, typography, photography and motion shape the experience together. We use interaction to guide attention and express personality, while keeping the important information accessible. Mobile layouts, readable contrast and reduced-motion behaviour are part of the design decisions. A cinematic first impression should lead naturally into pages that work.

Connect the website to the wider business

A website rarely works alone. Content, advertising, enquiries and reporting need a consistent journey. We consider search visibility, page structure and analytics during development, and define integrations around the project’s needs. Whether the implementation uses a content management system or a custom front end, the handover should make publishing and future changes understandable.

What the scope can include

For brands launching a new presence, replacing a dated website or bringing several disconnected pages into one clearer experience.

  • Website structure and user journeys
  • Responsive UI and visual design
  • Page development and content integration
  • Technical SEO and analytics foundations
  • Testing, launch planning and handover

Questions before we begin

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. The starting point can be an audit of the current structure, design and technical limitations. We then identify what can be retained and what needs to change.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of the development scope. Key journeys should be reviewed at representative phone, tablet and desktop sizes before a production launch.

Can our team update the content?

The publishing approach is chosen around your team’s needs. Editable content, approval steps and handover requirements should be agreed before development begins.
