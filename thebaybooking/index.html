Before you book · important play rules

Play tickets exclude the swimming pool. An accompanying adult must stay in the building. No outside food is allowed, including for peanut-allergy safety. Sessions have fixed start and finish times.

Bookwhen’s daytime listing describes 1 hour 45 minutes of play. The after-school listing shows a longer window but repeats that duration; the owner must verify the actual end time before publishing.
