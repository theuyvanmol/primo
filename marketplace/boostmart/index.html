Before you launch

This is a standalone frontend demonstration, not a production marketplace. Login, registration, business management, payments, admin dashboards, genuine reviews and a database are not implemented.

All store names, descriptions and ratings are fictional. Store illustrations are emoji placeholders. Replace these with authorised content and photography before a commercial launch. Google Fonts load externally.

Domain, hosting, integrations and customisation are separate. Commercial licence, support and asset redistribution terms must be agreed with PRIMO before purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Does it require a framework build?

No. The supplied template uses HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript and can be served by static hosting.

Are the businesses real?

No. Names, descriptions and ratings are fictional demonstration data.

Are payments and accounts connected?

No. These require separate backend and authentication development.

Can the design be customised?

Yes. Edit the CSS variables, page content, sample business data and brand image.
